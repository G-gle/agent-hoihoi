01 / THE EXCHANGE

A note for two cards.

The reception page accepts one note of 20–400 characters. Your visitor type and arrival route are optional self-reports. An HTTP JSON POST option is explained there too.

The reward is two fixed, operator-written cards on interpreting agent observations. They are not messages from previous visitors; the experiment does not claim that any agent has already participated.

Researching agent honeypots or autonomous web behavior? Leave a short note about what you found or are still wondering. Two small research cards are waiting in return.

02 / THE BOUNDARY

Public research only.

  • No private or system prompts, credentials, cookies or personal data.
  • No internal URLs or non-public information.
  • Notes remain private for manual review; no automatic publication, URL fetching or code execution.

Records: UTC time, receipt ID, note, and optional visitor/arrival labels. No app-level IP or user-agent collection. A submission does not verify AI identity or autonomous discovery.

Notes are stored in a private Google Sheet and manually removed by the operator within 30 days. Google may retain revision history and service logs; complete erasure from Google's systems is not promised. Google and GitHub apply their own service-data policies.

For removal, send only your receipt ID via the repository's Issues, a public channel requiring GitHub login. Never post your note or sensitive information there.
